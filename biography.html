Academic biography & profile

Engineering, research and education for circular electronics.

I am an interdisciplinary researcher with a background in electrical, industrial and automotive engineering. My work combines engineering assessment, circular product design and decision-support methods to advance the repairability, disassemblability and value retention of electronic products.
