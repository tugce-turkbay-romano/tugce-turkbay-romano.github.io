01 · About

Research for a circular future.

I am a researcher working at the intersection of electrical engineering, circular economy and sustainable product design.

My multidisciplinary background spans automotive engineering, battery thermal management, industrial engineering and electrical engineering, which allows me to approach circularity challenges from technical, systems and decision-making perspectives.

I hold a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Université Grenoble Alpes. My research focuses on developing practical methods to assess product circularity and support decisions related to repair, reuse, remanufacturing, residual value and end-of-use management.

My work combines engineering analysis, life-cycle thinking, decision-support methods and product information to bridge product-level technical assessment with broader circular-economy implementation and performance measurement.
