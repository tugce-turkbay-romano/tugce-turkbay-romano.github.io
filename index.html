01 · About

Research for a circular future.

I am a researcher working at the intersection of electrical engineering, circular economy and sustainable product design.

I hold a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Université Grenoble Alpes. My work spans product circularity, repairability, disassemblability, modularity, life cycle assessment and digital product information.

Research Engineer
G2Elab, Grenoble · February 2025–January 2026
