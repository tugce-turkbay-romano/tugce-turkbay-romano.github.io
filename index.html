Disassemblability assessment

Disassemblability Index for PCB-Based Electronic Circuits

Sustainability · 2024

The method adapts eDiM to PCB-level operations so designers can formulate measurable disassemblability requirements.

Problem
Existing disassembly metrics do not adequately capture PCB-level operations, including component removal and desoldering.
Method
Experimental disassembly analysis and adaptation of the ease of Disassembly Metric (eDiM) to PCB-based power electronic converters.
Evidence
Application to a power electronic converter, including component-level disassembly sequences and connection technologies.
Contribution
A PCB-based disassemblability assessment method with quantifiable requirements for circular product design.
Decision relevance
Supports connection-technology selection, product redesign, repair, refurbishment and remanufacturing decisions.

Repairability assessment

Repairability Index for PCB-Based Electronic Circuits

Journal of Remanufacturing · 2026

Five criteria—traceability, design features, disassembly/reassembly, diagnostics and spare parts—are complemented by warning indicators for critical barriers.

Problem
Aggregated repairability scores can conceal critical repair barriers and commonly treat the PCB as a replaceable unit rather than a repairable system.
Method
Repair-workflow modelling, analysis of existing indices, interviews with eight repair experts and a weighting survey involving 50 electronics and eco-design experts.
Evidence
Five weighted assessment categories, two PCB case studies and an open-access Excel assessment tool.
Contribution
A technically grounded PCB repairability index with warning indicators that prevent critical barriers from being hidden by the overall score.
Decision relevance
Helps repair professionals evaluate feasibility and identify bottlenecks while providing actionable eco-design feedback to manufacturers.

Circular modularity

Modularity Concept for PCB-Based Electronic Circuits

Procedia CIRP · 2023

Circular modularity requires more than modular circuit topology: modules should also be structurally separable and designed around their intended end-of-life strategy.

Problem
Power-electronic modularity is often driven by topology and performance, not structural needs for repair, reuse, upgrade and recycling.
Method
Conceptual review of functional and structural modularity across four converter architectures: PEBB, MIC, MMC and PCA.
Evidence
Comparison of existing PEC architectures with design recommendations for repair, reuse, upgrade and recycling.
Contribution
Circular-design requirements for defining modules by reliability, lifetime, value and material compatibility, using detachable, standardized interfaces.
Decision relevance
Guides module boundaries, component grouping and interconnections while exposing trade-offs among detachability, efficiency, cost and reliability.

Data for circular decisions

Data Requirements for R-Strategy Decisions on End-of-Use Electronics

Sustainability · 2026

Accessibility drops as the assessment moves from identification and compliance toward lifetime, testing and diagnostic evidence; missing Mandatory data leaves decisions unresolved rather than proving a strategy infeasible.

Problem
Choosing between reuse, repair, refurbishment, remanufacturing, repurposing, component reuse and recycling requires product information, yet it is often unclear which data are needed, where they come from and how they are used.
Method
An end-of-use decision-tree framework, formalized with IDEF0 and GRAI Net models, used to trace the data required at each activity and decision point, cross-referenced with literature indicators of availability, importance and sensitivity.
Evidence
Illustrative application to four end-of-use laptop adapters, with each dataset classified by accessibility and by necessity: Mandatory, Required for reliable assessment, or Optional.
Contribution
A decision-oriented map linking individual data requirements to specific end-of-use decisions and revealing where critical information gaps occur.
Decision relevance
Shows which information Digital Product Passports and collaborative data sharing should provide so that R-strategy decisions can actually be made.
